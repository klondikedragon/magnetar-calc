# Example ideas backlog

These are research leads, not published lessons. Each needs the authoring
playbook's source, implementation, and verification steps before promotion.

- Golden-ratio companions: Lucas, Pell, and related recurrences.
- n-bonacci families: tribonacci, tetranacci, and general k-step recurrence
  notation with efficient exact evaluation.
- Basement-number constructions: first resolve the intended definition and
  source terminology; several unrelated ideas circulate under similar names.
- Knuth and Conway notation: show exact small cases, structural larger cases,
  and clear distinctions among arrow counts.
- Steinhaus–Moser: source-linked polygonal constructions, Mega, and Moser's
  number, with visual notation and structural provenance.
- Fast-growing hierarchies: separately staged examples once ordinal semantics,
  structural representation, and source-backed notation are settled.
